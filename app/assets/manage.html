Some videos couldn't be added

The frame plays videos up to 1080p (Full HD). It converts "High Efficiency" (HEVC) videos by itself, and this page makes a smaller copy of 4K videos before sending them, but that needs a browser that can play the video and the page kept open while it does. Changing two camera settings on the phone makes every new video work as it is, with nothing to wait for:

iPhone

  • Settings → Camera → Formats → choose Most Compatible.
  • Settings → Camera → Record Video → choose 1080p (not 4K).

Samsung Galaxy

  • Camera → ⚙ Settings → Advanced video options → turn off High efficiency videos and HDR10+ video (10-bit HDR videos are too much for the frame).
  • Back in the camera, set the video size to FHD or 1080p (not UHD / 4K).

Google Pixel and other Android phones

  • Camera settings → Video → set the video codec to H.264, or turn off "More efficient video" / "H.265".
  • Set the resolution to 1080p.

A video you already took

Most phones' photo apps can save a smaller copy (on iPhone: share it as "Most Compatible"; on Android: Edit → Save as copy, or share it at a lower resolution). On a computer, the free HandBrake app's "Fast 1080p30" preset makes a file that works.
